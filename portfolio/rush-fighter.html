The interesting problem
C# ON THE WEB, IN AN ENGINE THAT DOESN'T DO THAT
01
Shipping C# to the browser
Godot 4 does not officially support exporting C# projects to the web. A jam game that nobody can play in a browser loses most of its audience, so rather than rewrite in GDScript or accept a downloadable build, we got the WebAssembly export working and wrote the procedure down in the repository so the next project doesn't rediscover it.
02
A second engine, on purpose
Six years of Unity is a comfortable place to stay. This is the second game this team has shipped in Godot in 2026, after mAscAr — deliberately picking up an unfamiliar engine under jam pressure, which is the fastest way to find out what you actually understand about engines and what you had just memorised about one of them.
The itch page declares “No generative AI was used” — the art, writing and audio are the team's own.
