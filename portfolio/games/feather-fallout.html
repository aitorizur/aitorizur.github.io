Tower DefenseGame JamUnityC#

A tower defense game where turrets can switch between two colours, each able to shoot only enemies of the matching colour. It adds a dynamic twist to the genre: you constantly re-tune your turrets based on which colour dominates the screen at any moment.

Developed in 2 weeks for 1-Bit Jam #4. The theme was "tower" and the limitation was that games could only show two colours on screen at any time.

Play it on itch.io ↗

CONTRIBUTIONS

Dynamic tower defense

I designed and programmed the core loop, where turrets flip between two colours and only damage matching enemies — turning colour management into the central tactical decision.

Paired turrets

Turrets work in colour pairs, so covering a lane means balancing both colours at once. I tuned their placement and switching rules to keep that trade-off constantly interesting.

In-game economy

I designed the round-based economy — DPS, effectiveness values and per-round budgets — so every purchase between waves is a meaningful decision.

Aesthetics

We committed to a strict two-colour palette and a matching setting and sound design, turning the jam's limitation into the game's identity.
